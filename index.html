How it works

"Your odds" on each gin is a weighted average of your own ratings: gins with a close flavor score count the most, "Loved it" counts higher than "Liked it", and "Not for me" pulls the odds down. Confidence rises as you rate more gins near it.

Each gin is scored 0 to 5 on eight flavors: juniper, citrus, floral, herbal, spice, earthy and woody, fruity and sweet, and savory or coastal. Matches are the gins with the closest scores, nudged by strength. Bar backups are the closest matches that most bars and liquor stores carry. "Gins you might like" ranks every gin you haven't rated: near the ones you loved (counted about double) or liked, and away from the ones marked "Not for me." Total Wine prices were checked on totalwine.com in November 2026 and vary by store.
